Directional market-stance pill with a trend icon — `bullish` (prairie green,
up arrow), `bearish` (amber, down arrow), or `neutral` (wheat tone, dash). Use
`size="lg"` (default) for hero placement and `size="sm"` inline. The label text
mirrors the stance, so it reads on its own.

## Examples

```tsx
<MarketStanceBadge stance="bullish" />
<MarketStanceBadge stance="bearish" size="sm" />
```
